How it works (it's not complicated)

Lots of people hang up on voicemail and call someone else. Missly keeps them talking until you can call back.

  1. Someone calls. You're busy.

    On a roof, driving, eating lunch. Your phone rings like normal first.

  2. Missly texts them

    Within seconds, in your business's name. Friendly, not robotic.

  3. It asks the stuff you'd ask

    Name, address, what's wrong, how urgent, when you can come by.

  4. You get the good stuff

    One text with everything you need to call them back. That's it.

What you get: a text, not another app

One clear text per lead. Every lead is also saved to a simple dashboard, for when you feel organized.

  • Urgent stuff flagged first. Leaks don't wait.
  • Up to 3 follow-ups if they go quiet. Polite, not clingy.
  • No texts at 2am. Never quotes prices for you. That's your job.
  • Callers can reply STOP anytime, and Missly actually stops.

When they ghost you, Missly doesn't take it personally

People get busy. Missly nudges them three times, nicely, then hands them back to you.

  1. 2 hours later

    A light nudge while they still remember calling.

    Hi again! We'd still love to help. Is it a repair, a leak, or a new roof?

    Only during business hours. Never late at night.

  4. Then it's your turn

    You get a “worth a call” alert with everything Missly learned, so you can pick up the phone. If they reply at any point, Missly picks the conversation right back up.

Questions people actually ask

Do I need a new phone number?

Nope. Keep your number. You dial one short code from your business phone so calls you don't answer go to Missly. Takes about 2 minutes, and we send instructions for your exact phone company.

Will customers know it's automated?

Texts come from your business name and read like a helpful office assistant. If someone asks, Missly tells the truth: it's your automated assistant. If they want a real human, it pings you right away.

Is it allowed to text callers?

Missly only texts people who called you first, identifies your business in every conversation, and stops immediately if someone replies STOP. See our SMS Terms.

Is there a contract?

No. Month to month. Cancel anytime, no breakup speech required.

Is it only for roofers?

We started with roofers because they're always on roofs. It works for any local business that misses calls: dental offices, HVAC, plumbers, you name it.

What if it says something weird?

It sticks to your services, hours, and area, and never quotes prices. You can tell it anything it should always or never say when you sign up.
