How it works

Callers who reach voicemail often hang up and try someone else. Missly keeps the conversation going until you can call back.

  1. A call goes unanswered

    You're on a job, driving, or it's after hours. The call rings through as normal first.

  2. Missly texts them in seconds

    The text comes from your business name and sounds like your office, not a robot.

  3. It asks what they need

    Name, address, the problem, how urgent it is, and when you can come take a look.

  4. You get a summary

    A text lands on your phone with everything you need to call back. Missly follows up if they go quiet.

What lands on your phone

No app to check. You get one clear text per lead, and every lead is saved to a simple dashboard.

  • Urgent jobs, like active leaks, are flagged first
  • Up to three polite follow-ups if the caller stops replying
  • No texts late at night, and never quotes prices for you
  • Callers can reply STOP anytime

Questions

Do I need a new phone number?

No. You keep the number your customers already know. We help you set up call forwarding so unanswered calls go through Missly. It takes about 15 minutes.

Will customers know it's automated?

Texts come from your business name and read like a helpful office assistant. If someone asks, Missly is upfront that it's your automated text assistant. If a caller asks for a person, Missly alerts you right away so you can call back.

Is it allowed to text callers?

Missly only texts people who called you first, identifies your business in every conversation, and stops immediately if someone replies STOP. See our SMS Terms.

Is there a contract?

No. It's month to month, and you can cancel anytime.

Is it only for roofers?

Roofers are who we started with, but it works for any local business that misses calls, including dental offices and other home service trades.
