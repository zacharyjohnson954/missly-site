Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 15, 2026

Missly, a service of Johnson Exteriors LLC ("Missly," "we," "us," "our") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your choices. It applies to our website, phone and text-message communications, and the services we provide to homeowners and to businesses.

Information we collect

  • Contact information such as your name, phone number, email address and service address, when you call, text, email or fill out a form.
  • Request details you share about the service you need, your preferred times, and similar information.
  • Communication records, including call logs and the content of text messages exchanged with us or with businesses that use our services.
  • Basic technical information from website visits, such as browser type and pages viewed.

How we use information

  • To respond to your inquiry, answer questions, and schedule service or a callback.
  • To send text messages related to your inquiry, including follow-ups and reminders.
  • To provide our services to the businesses you contact, including logging your request and notifying their staff.
  • To operate, maintain and improve our services, and to comply with legal obligations.

Text messaging and mobile information

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties. Information may be shared only with service providers that help us deliver our messaging service (for example, our telecommunications and software providers), and only for that purpose.

You can opt out of text messages at any time by replying STOP. Reply HELP for help. Message frequency varies, and message and data rates may apply. See our SMS Terms.

How we share information

We do not sell your personal information. We share information only:

  • With the business you contacted, so they can respond to your request.
  • With service providers that perform services for us (such as phone, text messaging, hosting and software providers), under obligations to protect it.
  • When required by law, or to protect the rights and safety of our customers, the public, or us.

Data retention and security

We keep information only as long as needed for the purposes above or as required by law, and we use reasonable safeguards to protect it. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

Your choices

You may ask to access, correct or delete your information by contacting us below. You may opt out of text messages at any time by replying STOP.

Children

Our services are not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date shows when it last changed.
