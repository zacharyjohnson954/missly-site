SMS Terms & Conditions

Last updated: November 15, 2026

Program description

Missly, a service of Johnson Exteriors LLC, operates a customer-care text messaging program for itself and for the local businesses it serves. When you call one of these businesses and the call is missed, you hear a short message that a text is on the way, and you may receive text messages about your inquiry: questions about what you need, scheduling, appointment follow-ups, and replies to your messages. Texts identify the business you called.

How you opt in

You opt in by calling the business's phone number. We do not text anyone who has not called first. Consent to receive texts is not a condition of any purchase.

Message frequency

Message frequency varies based on your conversation, typically a few messages per inquiry, including up to three follow-ups if you don't reply.

Costs

Message and data rates may apply. Check your mobile plan for details.

How to opt out

Reply STOP at any time to stop receiving messages. You'll receive one confirmation message and no further texts unless you opt in again by replying START.

Help

Reply HELP for help, or contact us at (480) 353-0935 or zach@missly.io.

Carriers

Mobile carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

Privacy

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. See our Privacy Policy for details.
